KEY VERIFICATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────
1. State Ownership: Module 6 populates state.executive_summary (not state.final_report). Module 7's Orchestrator retains ownership of final report assembly.
2. Architecture & Truth: Population of separate workflow state fields (risk_findings, missing_information, follow_ups, executive_summary) rather than container mirroring.
3. Multi-dimensional Risk: Synthesises risks from financial, legal, operational, and compliance domains across extracted terms, missing terms, and compliance failures.
4. Evidence Traceability: Every risk finding validates referenced EV-NNN evidence IDs against state.evidence_registry.
5. Deterministic Resilience: Fully operational offline via deterministic fallback if LLM synthesis is unavailable or fails schema validation.

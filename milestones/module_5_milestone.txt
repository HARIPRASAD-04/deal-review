────────────────────────────────────────────
FEEDBACK LOOP ARCHITECTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Initial Extraction:
   TermExtractionAgent extracts initial terms from evidence registry.

2. Compliance Review:
   ComplianceReviewAgent evaluates policy rules. If a rule produces INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE,
   generate_clarification_handoffs() creates a Handoff (CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED)
   directed to term_extraction.

3. Handoff Router:
   HandoffRouter inspects pending_handoffs.
   - If clarification_attempts[rule_id] >= max_attempts:
     Resolves handoff with escalation notes, creates human escalation item,
     emits LOOP_LIMIT_REACHED audit event.
   - Otherwise:
     Dispatches to TermExtractionAgent.clarify() for targeted re-extraction.
     If new terms are found:
       Updates state.extracted_terms
       Dispatches to ComplianceReviewAgent.review_rule() for immediate re-check.
       Updates state.compliance_results.
